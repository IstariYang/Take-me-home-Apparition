## Description

- **Take Me Home, Apparition - All Vanilla, Hearthfire & CC Homes** is the ultimate evolution of the Apparition spell series. Unlike previous versions that served a single player home, this edition connects you to 18 homes across Skyrim, including all vanilla purchaseable houses, all three Hearthfire homesteads, and all Creation Club properties.

- **A single spell is all you need** — cast to go home, cast again to return. One spell, unlimited round trips between the wilderness and every sanctuary you own.

- The moment you acquire your first home — whether by purchasing the key, completing a quest, or building it yourself — a spell tome materializes in your pack. Once learned, this spell allows you to apparate to any home you own from anywhere in the world.

- When you are safely within your sanctuary, casting the spell again offers you a choice: return to where you left off in the wilderness, or travel to another home you own.

- The spell is designed to feel like the Apparition from a certain famous wizarding world — a swirl of twisting invisibility as you vanish, and a burst of particles as you arrive at your destination. (Great Thanks to [Kittytail](https://www.nexusmods.com/profile/Kittytail))

## How it works

- ***In the wilderness***: Cast to record your current position, then choose from a list of Holds and homes to apparate to.

- ***Inside a home***: Cast to return to your last wilderness position, or select another home to travel to.

- ***First home bonus***: The spell tome is automatically granted when you acquire your very first home. No need to hunt for it.

## Feature

- **18 homes supported out of the box** (5 vanilla + 3 Hearthfire + 2 CC key + 7 CC quest)

- **Intelligent detection** knows whether you are indoors, outdoors, or even in your backyard.

- **Two-tier menu system** — select by Hold, then by specific home.

- **Clean and optimized** — single-pass menu building, cached data, and lightweight polling.

## Requirements

- SKSE64 (2.0.20+)
- SkyUI (5.2+)
- UIExtensions
- Backported Extended

*Hearthfires DLC and Anniversary Edition CC content are optional. The mod will safely skip any homes you don't have installed.*

## Installation

*Install with your preferred mod manager. Load order should place this mod after all three requirements listed above.*
